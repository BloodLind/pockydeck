# Code of conduct

PockyDeck welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep feedback specific to the work. Explain disagreements with evidence. Help newcomers find the information they need, and respect other people's time and privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests, or other spaces maintained by this project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected people. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome.

To report a conduct problem, contact the repository owner through an available private contact method on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/BloodLind). If no private channel is listed, open a brief issue asking for private contact without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details. Maintainers should avoid sharing reports beyond what is necessary to address them.
