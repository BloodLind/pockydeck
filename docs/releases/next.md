# Stabilization release (unreleased)

Current source removes Shizuku and running-process indicators. A blue dot with a dark outline on Home identifies the last ROM successfully launched on each console. Focusing or hovering a game shows a matching blue Last played on [platform] tag above its title. The existing launch history persists it across restarts; failed/cancelled launches do not replace it.

PS2 boot-path launches recreate the emulator task with the newly selected game. Save first: task recreation can discard unsaved emulation. This uses Android activity lifecycle and does not force-stop another application.

Collections use card widths derived from available space and density, with consistent gaps. UI/card-size settings use controller-aware sliders. Confirm enters adjustment; D-pad Left/Right adjusts, Back or Confirm finishes, and Up/Down moves away. Switching from touch to controller restores the exact touched control. Uncached covers in large collections pause during scrolling, retain displayed artwork, and resume after 180 ms of rest. Cached covers display immediately, Home preloads all twenty possible cards including offscreen artwork, and Android icons bypass the cover idle gate. Home cover buckets are capped at 512 px so the entire row fits within 20 MiB of the shared 32 MiB cache. Bounded image caches survive normal app switches and respond to memory pressure. Decodes are paced and sized to the display bucket, file metadata stays off the UI thread, and fresh covers use a 160 ms eased cartridge-like slide/fade; cached covers and Reduce motion skip the reveal. Held-repeat timing counts dispatch work toward the interval without replaying overdue input.

List mode keeps selection and controller focus in the left list. The right pane contains cover art, console, format, favorite status and availability, without buttons. Y toggles the selected item’s favorite state, Select opens Details and extra actions, and Start retains the menu. Touching a row selects it and publishes footer actions, including on taps that finish before input-mode recomposition. Y after touch restores that exact row. Grid artwork is bounded by the content height so enlarged UI settings leave room for both title lines above the dock.

The mapped Back button now provides a Controls shortcut from any collection item to All filters, without scrolling back to the top. Left/Right reaches layout and sorting; Down or Back returns to the same game and viewport. The shortcut works in grid and list layouts across Library, Apps and Favorites, follows the configured A/B mapping, and cancels pending grid movement to prevent delayed focus jumps. The footer labels the shortcut Controls and keeps modal dismissal separate.

List rows now use 56 reference-dp artwork and a single-line title, while keeping full titles in the preview and minimum touch targets. The redundant Start/Menu footer hint is removed. **Display → List artwork background** independently enables the same cached blur and quick reveal as Home behind ROM selections in lists; it defaults off and never appears in grid mode, Search, Details or Settings.

Navigation dock and action-footer taps use shape-matched press fills instead of rectangular default ripples. Dock icons stay circular and footer actions use rounded pills. Feedback appears immediately on press and clears in 50 ms; Reduce motion removes the release animation.

Shell bands now follow their contents: at the Flip2 baseline, status/dock/footer reserve 32/60/48 dp instead of screen-height percentages. This returns about 106 physical pixels to the page and brings headers closer to the top bar. The dock adds padding above, below and around its end buttons, preserving circle/icon sizes and keeping the capsule clear of page content and footer actions. Start uses compact measured typography, and Select is a small circular overlapping-panel symbol matching the A/X/Y hints. The action label remains visible and touch targets stay at least 48 dp. Android's native sans-serif replaces the geometric UI face, with tabular status numerals and quieter metadata. Available storage is grouped under Free with distinct drive/SD icons and preserved unknown/multiple-volume semantics.

Home keeps its previous title and card-row anchors independently of that compact shell. This restores the familiar composition while collection/settings pages retain their extra space and all pages keep the revised typography and compact status/footer controls.

Footer readability is restored with larger semibold labels and clearer legends. Its background now hugs the actions instead of drawing a full-width empty strip, retaining the same 48 dp touch allocations. Navigation button/icon artwork is about 10% larger without moving the Home title or cards. The default backdrop now adds a soft purple tint to the charcoal base. Display offers five color presets, tint strength, and grain intensity, defaulting to Purple/40%/30%. A periodic smoothing filter removes sharp speckles from the matte texture. Intensity tiles are cached with a 640 KiB maximum, with no file loading or per-frame random generation; 0% grain removes the texture.

ROM-folder settings preserve the visible row and its offset while scans update progress, warnings, unidentified-game controls, and discovered folders. Separate keyed rows provide stable scrolling in both the dedicated ROM folders section and compact All settings; scan updates do not request initial focus.

Home launcher settings offer **Change Home launcher** when PockyDeck holds the role, opening Android's Home chooser so another launcher can be selected. RetroArch core rows appear only for consoles whose selected or sole compatible emulator uses cores.

**Display → Home artwork background** optionally shades Home with the selected ROM’s blurred artwork. It defaults off and persists independently of other display preferences. A separately cached thumbnail (maximum 128 px edge) reuses decoded cover pixels when available and is blurred off the UI thread, independently of the cover queue. It updates after Home's single navigation settling delay, with a 120 ms entrance/crossfade that keeps the prior image visible until the next is ready. Navigation does not skip the reveal. The effect is Home-only, decorative, and motion-aware; apps or missing artwork retain the standard background.

Controls offer original 18–72 ms clicks and pops with matched RMS levels, a 0–100% launcher volume slider, and independently switchable Android haptic feedback. Movement and card focus now share one dry 18 ms click without the previous falling tone. A 40 ms navigation guard follows the full 55 ms accelerated D-pad cadence without abruptly halving the sound rate at 80 ms. Other actions retain their 80 ms minimum; all cues reserve their full duration plus margin. Native audio runs on a dedicated worker with one pending cue at most; busy and stale requests are dropped. Reducing volume/muting updates the active voice; increasing volume never boosts or revives its tail, including when settings change during a slow native call.

Repeated-feedback loudness is also limited by recent sound energy. Cue duration normalizes the matched-RMS samples; a worker-owned 200 ms energy decay with 5% burst headroom attenuates dense repeats. A burst can only stay at its current gain or get quieter, including when taps are uneven or cues alternate. After 600 ms without a new cue the normal cue level returns. The volume slider and independent haptics remain available.

Vibration now marks the beginning and end of an input burst rather than each repeated sound. Resolved controller holds keep the burst open even through the 650 ms trigger repeat delay. Rapid taps share the same burst, which closes after 160 ms without another action following release. Single actions receive one pulse. Disabling vibration, entering the keyboard or leaving the app cancels any pending end pulse; sound and volume remain independent.

Android displays PockyDeck with a reference-inspired white pd monogram, blue/red centers, and a dark navy background. Native vector foreground/background layers support adaptive masks and Android themed icons; an editable SVG lives in `docs/branding/pockydeck-icon.svg`. The package ID stays `dev.handheld.launcher`; a matching signing certificate remains necessary for updates.
